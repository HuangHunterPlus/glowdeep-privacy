1. About this policy

This policy explains how Glowdeep and its integrated services handle information. For privacy questions, contact the Glowdeep developer at qiao31495@gmail.com.

2. Game data on your device

Glowdeep does not require a separate game account. Game progress, settings, purchase entitlements, limited gameplay event records, and diagnostic logs are stored locally to operate the game, restore progress, prevent duplicate purchase rewards, and help troubleshoot issues. The game's own gameplay and diagnostic records are not automatically uploaded to a developer-operated server. Android backup and restoration may handle app data according to your device and Google account settings.

3. Advertising and privacy choices

Glowdeep integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for optional rewarded advertisements and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable consent choices. Test releases may display test advertisements; the advertising SDK is still a third-party service.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares information such as IP addresses (which may be used to estimate general location), device or account identifiers, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Actual processing depends on SDK configuration, consent, and device settings. Ad services may communicate with Google when initialized or when an ad is loaded, before you choose to watch it.

Where applicable, the app presents a consent form. You can access available consent controls through the game's Privacy Options and manage advertising controls in your Android or Google settings. You may choose not to watch rewarded advertisements.

4. In-app purchases

Available in-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. The app handles product identifiers, purchase status, purchase tokens, and transaction information to deliver and restore purchased entitlements. We do not receive your full payment card details. Google processes payment and related account information under its own policies.

5. Support and sharing

If you email us or voluntarily send a diagnostic summary, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond and investigate your request. Please do not include passwords or payment card details. Information is also processed by Google services as described above, and may be disclosed where required by law.

6. Retention, deletion, and security

Local data remains until it is removed or overwritten. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes local app data, but does not cancel purchases or delete Google's records or existing backups. Manage backups through your device or Google account. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations; contact us to request deletion.

Google determines retention and deletion for data it processes under its policies. We use reasonable safeguards for information we handle, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

7. Your rights and children

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of personal information, or to withdraw consent. Contact us about information you have provided directly; use Google's privacy controls for information handled by Google.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

8. Third-party policies and international processing

Google may process information on servers outside your country, as explained in its policies. Please review Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps using its services, and Google's data and privacy controls.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the game or its services change. Updates will be posted here with a revised effective date. Contact: qiao31495@gmail.com.

2. 设备上的游戏数据

Glowdeep 无需注册独立游戏账号。游戏进度、设置、购买权益、有限的游玩事件记录和诊断日志保存在本地，用于运行游戏、恢复进度、防止重复发放购买奖励和排查问题。游戏自身的游玩及诊断记录不会自动上传到开发者运营的服务器。Android 系统备份和恢复可能根据你的设备及 Google 账号设置处理应用数据。

3. 广告与隐私选择

Glowdeep 集成 Google AdMob（Google Mobile Ads SDK），提供可选择观看的激励广告，并使用 Google 用户消息平台（UMP）管理适用的隐私同意选择。测试版本可能展示测试广告，但广告 SDK 仍属于第三方服务。

Google Mobile Ads SDK 会收集和共享 IP 地址（可能用于推断大致位置）、设备或账号标识符、广告互动及诊断信息等数据，用于广告投放、效果衡量、分析和反欺诈。实际处理情况取决于 SDK 配置、同意状态及设备设置。广告服务可能在初始化或加载广告时就与 Google 通信，早于你选择观看广告的时间。

在适用情况下，应用会展示隐私同意表单。你可以通过游戏的“隐私选项”访问可用的同意控制，也可以在 Android 或 Google 设置中管理广告选项。你可以选择不观看激励广告。

4. 应用内购买

可用的应用内购买通过 Google Play Billing 处理。应用会处理商品标识、购买状态、购买令牌和交易信息，以发放和恢复已购买权益。我们不会收到你的完整银行卡信息。Google 根据其自身政策处理支付及相关账号信息。

5. 用户支持与信息共享

如果你通过邮件联系我们或主动发送诊断摘要，我们会收到你的邮箱地址及你选择提供的信息，用于回复和排查问题。请勿发送密码或银行卡信息。Google 服务也会按上述说明处理信息；在法律要求时，相关信息可能被披露。

6. 保存、删除与安全

本地数据会保留至被删除或覆盖。清除应用存储或卸载应用可以移除本地应用数据，但不会取消购买，也不会删除 Google 保存的记录或已有备份。请通过设备或 Google 账号管理备份。我们仅在处理请求和履行适用义务所需的时间内保留支持邮件，你可以联系我们申请删除。

Google 依据其政策决定其所处理数据的保存与删除方式。我们对所处理的信息采取合理保护措施，但任何存储或传输方式都不能保证绝对安全。

7. 你的权利与儿童隐私

根据所在地区，你可能享有访问、更正、删除、限制使用个人信息或撤回同意等权利。对于你直接提供给我们的信息，请联系我们；对于 Google 处理的信息，请使用 Google 隐私管理工具。

如果你是家长或监护人，认为儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们，以便核实并采取适当措施。

8. 第三方政策与跨境处理

Google 可能按照其政策在你所在国家以外的服务器上处理信息。请参阅 Google 隐私政策、Google 如何使用来自其合作应用的信息以及 Google 数据和隐私管理工具。
